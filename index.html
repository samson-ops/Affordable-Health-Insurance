Why Work With an Independent Agent?

Insurance carriers pay licensed agents a commission that is already built into every plan's price. You pay the same premium whether you use us or go direct. The difference is that you get someone who has read the fine print, checks your doctors and prescriptions against each plan, and picks up the phone when you have a problem next year.

We represent multiple carriers, so our recommendation is based on what fits you, not on a single company's product line. If there's a plan in your area we can't offer, we'll tell you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free to use an agent?

Yes. The carrier pays our commission, and that commission is already included in every plan's premium. There is no markup and no separate fee.

Can I keep my current doctor?

Often, but networks change every year. We verify your specific doctors against each plan before you enroll rather than assuming.

How do subsidies work?

Premium tax credits are based on your household size and estimated income for the year and are applied to your monthly premium in advance. We help you estimate accurately and report changes so you don't owe money back at tax time.

I'm self-employed and my income varies. Can I still qualify?

Yes, and that describes many of our clients. We help you set a reasonable estimate and update it during the year. Premiums are usually tax-deductible for the self-employed; confirm with your tax preparer.

I'm turning 65. When should I start?

Reach out three to four months before your birthday month. That leaves time to compare Advantage and Supplement options and enroll in Part B without a late penalty.

Will you sell my information?

No. Your details go only to the carrier you choose to enroll with.
